OUR SERVICES

Land Information New Zealand Records

We conduct searches of the Land Information New Zealand records and provide copies of:

  • Current Record of Title - official real-time title showing any current interests with diagram/deposited plan
  • Historical Record of Title - official real-time current and historical data
  • Document/Interest - as registered on the title e.g. lease, caveat, easement, covenant
  • Plan - Deposited, Unit, Flat or Spatial plan
  • Apartment Records - Body Corporate rule changes; title; plan; easements
  • Nominal Search - Personal name/company searched to establish any current ownership
  • Prior Ownership Search - Personal name/company searched to establish any past ownership
  • Block Land Search - Bulk lots, mass land or mapped areas

Court Searches

On your behalf we facilitate due diligence searches of court records to establish records of civil nationwide proceedings at the High, Supreme, Appeal, District, Employment (including Employment Relations Authority) and Environment Courts under specified company or personal names. A quality assured written report is provided.

Why is it so important to search the Record of Title and registered interests?

In recent times it has become extremely important to conduct your due diligence when buying, selling or investing in real estate. This is so you know exactly who owns the property, if there are any covenants, easements, caveats or other restrictions on the title and to confirm the correct legal description ie lot and DP number and the area. It is interesting to note that the Real Estate Agents Authority suggests "a licensee needs to establish whether or not there are interests registered on the title such as covenants, caveats, easements, drainage rights etc that should be brought to the attention of interested parties. This shows the importance of searching the Record of Title and interests.
